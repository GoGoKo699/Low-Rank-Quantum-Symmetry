# Learning route

Start with a rank-two mixed state whose two unequal probabilities are exchanged by a known transformation. Its occupied subspace stays the same, yet the state changes. This distinction is the reason the algorithm retains spectral bands rather than just the whole support.

The single educational anchor is **John Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information** (2018). The route uses selected portions of the author's January 2018 manuscript; it does not require reading the whole book first.

| Reader goal | Route |

| Connect the textbook to this project | [Watrous bridge](WATROUS_BRIDGE.md): section mapping, notation, a complete two-dimensional example and the missing project-specific steps |

| Follow the mathematical reduction and full implementation | [Spectral reduction](../science/spectral_reduction.md), then [finite copy algorithm](../science/copy_algorithm.md) |
| Understand reach, attribution and evidence | [Interpretation](../science/interpretation.md), [related work](../references/related_work.md) and [verification](../verification/README.md) |

The milestone map is shared understanding material. Its dependencies and targets follow the scientific argument; it contains no learner assessment or progress record. The bridge explains the foundations and connections, while the canonical scientific pages own the proofs, assumptions, constants and resource bounds.

The resulting capability is to learn an entire exact conjugation subgroup from independent low-rank mixed-state copies with arbitrary spectrum, under the supplied action and separation promise. A finished reading route does not establish independent verification of the theorem or mastery of every technical calculation.
